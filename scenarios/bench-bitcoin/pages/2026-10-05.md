## Headlines

1. **Bitcoin starts October above $62,000 as traders return from holidays** (Block Times). Spot volumes picked up on Monday after a quiet week, with most of the buying in the US session.
2. **Ethereum developers set a date for the next network upgrade** (The Hash Daily). Core developers agreed on a test schedule; the main network date depends on two more test runs.
3. **Solana network processes a record number of transactions** (Block Times). Most of the traffic came from small payments and games.
4. **Wallet maker recalls a batch of hardware devices** (The Hash Daily). The company said a faulty screen, not the security chip, was the problem.
5. **Mining difficulty set to rise at the next adjustment** (Coin Street Journal). The estimate points to an increase of about four percent.
6. **Exchange lists three new trading pairs against the euro** (Ledger Wire). The pairs open for trading on Thursday.
