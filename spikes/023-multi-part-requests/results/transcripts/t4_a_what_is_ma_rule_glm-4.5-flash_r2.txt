A moving average is a widely used technical analysis tool that smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price over a specific time period. It helps identify trends by filtering out short-term price fluctuations.

Key characteristics:
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA)**: Calculates the average closing price over a set number of periods (e.g., 20-day SMA)
- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**: Gives more weight to recent prices, making it more responsive to new information
- **Purpose**: Helps identify trend direction, support/resistance levels, and potential trend reversals
- **Common periods**: 10, 20, 50, 100, and 200 days for stocks; often shorter periods (like 9, 21) for cryptocurrencies

Moving averages are commonly used in trading strategies like crossovers (when short-term MA crosses above/below long-term MA) and as dynamic support/resistance levels.
