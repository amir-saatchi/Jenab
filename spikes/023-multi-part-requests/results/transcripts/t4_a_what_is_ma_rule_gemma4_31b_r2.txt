[12:00:04 assistant] A moving average is a technical analysis tool that smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price. It helps filter out short-term "noise" (random price fluctuations) to reveal the underlying trend of an asset.

**How it works:**
It calculates the average price over a specific number of periods (e.g., 20 days, 50 days, or 200 days). As a new price point is added, the oldest one is dropped, causing the average to "move" along the timeline.

**Common types:**
*   **Simple Moving Average (SMA):** The arithmetic mean of the prices over the period.
*   **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** Gives more weight to recent prices, making it more responsive to new information.
