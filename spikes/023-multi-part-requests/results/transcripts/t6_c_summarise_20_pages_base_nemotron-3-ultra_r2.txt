1. **MiCA Transition Period Ends 2026-07-01**

   - Issuers of asset-referenced tokens (ARTs) and e-money tokens (EMTs) must hold reserves in EU-authorised credit institutions
   - Reserves must be 1:1 backed, segregated, and invested in low-risk assets
   - Significant stablecoins face stricter capital and liquidity requirements

3. **Travel Rule (Transfer of Funds Regulation)**
   - Applies to transfers **above €1,000**
   - Originator and beneficiary information must accompany transfers
   - Applies to CASP-to-CASP, CASP-to-self-hosted, and self-hosted-to-CASP transfers

   - Includes verification of wallet ownership (e.g., signing a message, Satoshi test)

- **Compliance Infrastructure**: Build travel rule compliance (IVMS 101 messaging, counterparty verification)

- **Wallet Integrations**: Prepare systems to handle self-hosted wallet verification flows
