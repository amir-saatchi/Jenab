A moving average is a widely used technical analysis tool that smooths out price data by creating a constantly updated average price over a specific time period. Here are the key points:

**Basic Concept:**
- It calculates the average of a set of prices over a defined number of periods
- As new data becomes available, the oldest data point is dropped from the calculation
- This creates a "moving" line that follows price trends while smoothing out short-term fluctuations

**Common Types:**
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA)**: Equal weight given to all data points in the period
- **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**: Gives more weight to recent data points, making it more responsive to new information

**Common Time Periods:**
- Short-term: 10-day, 20-day
- Medium-term: 50-day, 100-day  
- Long-term: 200-day

**Uses in Trading:**
- Identify trend direction (upward/downward)
- Support and resistance levels
- Crossover signals (when short-term MA crosses above/below long-term MA)
- Smooth out price volatility

For your crypto watch project, moving averages could be very useful for analyzing BTC and ETH price trends, helping to filter out daily noise and identify longer-term market direction.
