**Proof of Work vs Proof of Stake:**

**Proof of Work (PoW)** is the original consensus mechanism used by Bitcoin. In PoW, miners compete to solve complex mathematical puzzles using computational power. The first miner to solve the puzzle gets to add a new block to the blockchain and receives a reward. This requires massive amounts of energy and specialized hardware.

**Proof of Stake (PoS)** is more energy-efficient. Instead of miners, validators are chosen to create new blocks based on how much cryptocurrency they "stake" (lock up) as collateral. Validators are rewarded for honest participation and penalized for malicious behavior. Ethereum transitioned from PoW to PoS with "The Merge" in 2022.

The key differences:
- Energy consumption: PoW uses massive energy, PoS is much more efficient
- Hardware requirements: PoW needs specialized mining rigs, PoS just requires staked ETH
- Security model: PoW relies on computational power, PoS relies on economic incentives
- Block creation: PoW uses competition, PoS uses selection based on stake size
