## Turn 1

**User:** What is a moving average?

**Agent:** A moving average is a calculation used to analyze data points by creating a series of averages of different subsets of the full data set. In finance and crypto, it is primarily used to smooth out short-term price fluctuations (noise) to identify the underlying trend of an asset's price over time.

There are two common types:
*   **Simple Moving Average (SMA):** The arithmetic mean of a set of prices over a specific period (e.g., the average closing price of the last 20 days).
*   **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** Similar to SMA, but it gives more weight to recent prices, making it more responsive to new information.
