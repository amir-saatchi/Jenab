## Turn 1

**User:** What is a moving average?

**Agent:** A moving average is a calculation used to analyze data points by creating a series of averages of different subsets of the full data set. In finance and crypto, it is primarily used to smooth out short-term price fluctuations (noise) to identify longer-term trends.

The most common types are:

*   **Simple Moving Average (SMA):** The average price over a specific number of periods (e.g., a 20-day SMA is the sum of the closing prices for the last 20 days divided by 20).
*   **Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** Similar to the SMA, but it gives more weight to recent prices, making it react faster to new price changes.

For example, if a coin's price is trending above its 50-day moving average, it is often interpreted as being in an uptrend.
