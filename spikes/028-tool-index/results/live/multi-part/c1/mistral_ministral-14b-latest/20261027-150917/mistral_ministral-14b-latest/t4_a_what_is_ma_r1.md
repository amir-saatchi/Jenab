## Turn 1

**User:** What is a moving average?

**Agent:** A **moving average (MA)** is a statistical tool used to analyze data points by creating a series of averages of different subsets of the full data set. It is commonly used in finance, economics, and time-series analysis to smooth out short-term fluctuations and highlight longer-term trends.

### Key Points:
1. **Smoothing**: Moving averages help reduce noise in data, making trends easier to identify.
2. **Types**:
   - **Simple Moving Average (SMA)**: Calculated by taking the average of a fixed number of past data points. For example, a 10-day SMA is the average of the last 10 days' prices.
   - **Exponential Moving Average (EMA)**: Gives more weight to recent data points, making it more responsive to new information.
3. **Applications**:
   - In finance, moving averages are used to identify trends, generate trading signals (e.g., crossover strategies), and spot potential reversals.
   - In your **Crypto Watch** project, a moving average could help analyze price trends for coins like BTC or ETH over time.

### Example:
If you have daily closing prices for BTC:
- A **10-day SMA** would be the average of the last 10 days' closing prices.
- A **50-day SMA** would average the last 50 days' prices, providing a broader trend view.
