# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. Jenab is still in design, so the most useful contributions right now are comments on the [SPEC](Docs/SPEC.md) and the open questions in the [Gates](Docs/Gates). Please open an issue before starting larger work. [DEVELOPMENT.md](Docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) explains how to build and test.

## Sign your commits (DCO)

Contributions are accepted under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/). By adding a sign-off line to each commit, you confirm that you wrote the change, or have the right to submit it, under the project's [Apache 2.0](LICENSE) license:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

`git commit -s` adds it for you. Pull requests with unsigned commits can't be merged.
